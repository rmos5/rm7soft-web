Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Overview

RoutineRun is a timed routine app for Android and Windows by rm7soft. This policy describes local app data, sharing, RoutineRun-related website pages, and support.

Routine Data

RoutineRun stores plans, routines, imported collections, custom audio, settings, and session state locally on your device. Importing and sharing use files that you choose through your device's file picker or sharing tools.

Exports can contain routine text, collection metadata, and included audio. Review the content before sharing it. The app also keeps local diagnostic logs, which you can choose to share when requesting support.

If account, sync, API, or cloud features are introduced, we will update this policy to describe how routine data is stored, processed, and protected.

Support Information

If you contact support, we may receive your email address and details you include, such as your device, platform, app version, screenshots, and issue description. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is needed to resolve your request.

Website Hosting

RoutineRun pages are hosted through rm7soft website infrastructure. Hosting and security providers may process technical request data to serve pages, route traffic, and protect the site.

Analytics and Advertising

This first version of the RoutineRun website is designed without advertising cookies or third-party analytics scripts. If analytics or advertising features are added, we will update this policy before launch.

Contact

For privacy questions about RoutineRun, contact routinerun@rm7soft.com.

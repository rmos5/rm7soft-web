Get help with RoutineRun

Questions about installation, timed routines, plans, or imports? Contact us for help, bug reports, and feedback.

Contact support

routinerun@rm7soft.com

Email RoutineRun support Request preview access

Include These Details

Android device model, Android version, RoutineRun version if known, what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead.

Trying the preview

RoutineRun is in development. Request preview access, or use the download button there after approval.

A Google account is required. Once approved, sign in with that account and follow the installation instructions in our email. Android 6.0+ and ARM64 are supported; ask about x64 (x86_64) availability.

Updating an older preview? Contact us before uninstalling — you could lose saved routines and settings.

Using music or video

Start your routine, then open your music or video app. Try a short routine first to check that you can hear the cues alongside your media; audio behavior can vary by device and app. Use vibration cues if you prefer, and check the background timer settings below.

Android background timers

Battery optimization can interrupt timers or delay alerts when the screen is off. Allow background activity and disable battery optimization for RoutineRun in your phone settings. Setting names vary by device.

Use the active run notification to pause or continue a routine, or choose Stop and close to end it.

Importing and sharing

Use RoutineRun exports to move routines, plans, or collections between installations. Collections imported from another creator are read-only; copy a routine into your own plan when you want to adapt it.

Reporting a problem

Tell us whether the issue happens while editing, running, importing, or sharing. For timer or sound issues, include whether the screen was off and which audio or vibration cues you were using.

If support asks for diagnostic logs, use the app's log sharing option and review what you send. Avoid including sensitive routine content in screenshots or attachments.

Install on Android

The Android APK requires Android 6.0 or later and ARM64 app support. It does not support 32-bit Android or x86 devices.

  1. Open the Google Drive link in the RoutineRun release section and choose Download. No Google account is needed.
  2. If Google Drive says it cannot scan this file for viruses, check that the filename matches the release details, then choose Download anyway to save the APK.
  3. Open the downloaded APK from your browser or Files app.
  4. If Android requests permission to install apps from this source, allow it for the browser or Files app you are using, then return to the installer.
  5. Choose Install, then open RoutineRun. You can turn off the source's installation permission afterward.

For updates, download the newer APK and install it over your existing copy. Android requires the same app identity and signing certificate. If it reports a conflict, contact support before uninstalling: uninstalling can remove locally stored routines and settings.

Android background timers

Battery optimization can interrupt timers or delay alerts when the screen is off. Allow background activity and disable battery optimization for RoutineRun in your phone settings. Setting names vary by device.

Use the active run notification to pause or continue a routine, or choose Stop and close to end it.

Importing and sharing

Use RoutineRun exports to move routines, plans, or collections between installations. Collections imported from another creator are read-only; copy a routine into your own plan when you want to adapt it.

Reporting a problem

Tell us whether the issue happens while editing, running, importing, or sharing. For timer or sound issues, include whether the screen was off and which audio or vibration cues you were using.

If support asks for diagnostic logs, use the app's log sharing option and review what you send. Avoid including sensitive routine content in screenshots or attachments.

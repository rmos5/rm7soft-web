Set up background launch

Launch a saved routine or plan without opening the app screen. Control it from the notification.

You'll need Android 8+, a launcher that supports pinned shortcuts, and notifications enabled for RoutineRun.

  1. In the app, pick a routine or plan. Choose Pin background shortcut for notification controls, or Pin app shortcut to open the run screen.
  2. Give your shortcut a name.
  3. Want playback to start on tap? Enable Start automatically. It's off by default. Routine shortcuts also offer Repeat.
  4. Confirm, then tap your new shortcut. Swipe down for the notification and press Play if automatic start is off.

Use other apps or turn the screen off. Expand the notification for Play/Pause, Previous, Next, Repeat, and Close. Pause before skipping sections or closing; Repeat works during playback. Tap the notification text to open the run screen.

Already running something? The shortcut keeps that run. Pause and close it before switching routines.

See what each shortcut icon means. Older shortcuts keep their old icons; pin them again for the new look.

Using music or video

Start a routine, then play your music or video. Try a short run to check cue volume — it varies by device and app. Adjust it in RoutineRun Settings or use vibration.

Android background timers

Timers stopping or alerts arriving late? Allow background activity and disable battery optimization for RoutineRun in your phone settings. Names vary by device.

Keep notifications enabled for background shortcuts and controls. To finish, pause and tap Close in the expanded notification.

Importing and sharing

Export routines, plans, or collections to share them. Other creators' collections are read-only. Copy a routine into your own plan to edit it.

Reporting a problem

Tell us when it happens. For timer or sound issues, mention your cues and whether the screen was off.

If we ask for logs, use the app's log sharing option. Check logs and screenshots for private details before sending.

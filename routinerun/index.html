Interested in trying RoutineRun?

RoutineRun is still in development and is not ready for general release. Potential employers and customers are welcome to request a preview to evaluate the app and the work behind it.

  1. Email us with your name. You may also include your organization and what you would like to evaluate; both are optional.
  2. Include your Android device or emulator model, Android version, and the email address associated with your Google account. If known, tell us whether you need ARM64 or x64 (x86_64).
  3. We review requests individually. If access is granted, we will reply with a private link and instructions for that build.

A Google account is required to download the preview. Access is granted to the account you provide; sign in with that account to open the private Google Drive link.

Email a preview request Open preview download

Already granted access? Open the ARM64 preview download above while signed in with your approved Google account. The link requires prior approval. If you received a separate x64 build, use the link in your email.

You can also write directly to routinerun@rm7soft.com. Sending a request does not automatically grant access.

The preview is Android-only and requires Android 6.0 or later. The current APK is for ARM64 devices. For Android x64 (x86_64) devices or emulators, request a compatible build and we will confirm availability. Preview builds may have bugs, and features may change.

From a single timer to a full plan

Give every step its moment.

Choose what happens, how long it takes, and the cue that guides you to the next step.

Flexible timing

Run steps one after another or together. Use countdown or count-up timers and mark the moments that matter.

Sound, speech & vibration

Add spoken instructions, built-in sounds, your own WAV audio, and vibration cues on supported devices.

Control your run

Pause and continue, repeat, change the run speed, or move to the previous or next section.

Plans that fit your day

Arrange routines into plans. Run the whole plan, start from a chosen routine, or run only the routines you select.

Create, reuse & share

Edit with copy, paste, undo, and redo. Import or share routines, plans, and collections as files.

Make it feel familiar

Choose English, Finnish, or Estonian and switch between light, dark, and rm7soft themes.

rm7soft Logo Variants

Ten quick vector directions for the parent rm7soft brand. These are intentionally simple and editable, so the best idea can be refined without fighting a bitmap logo.

01. Monogram Shield

strong favicon candidate

Compact and practical. Best if the brand needs to feel established and product-company serious.

02. Angled Seven Wordmark

cleanest header mark

The custom seven carries the identity while the lowercase wordmark stays calm and readable.

03. Modular Product Grid

multi-product signal

Small product tiles form a subtle seven, which fits the idea of a software catalog.

04. Dark Studio Lockup

premium software studio

More atmospheric and brand-like. Good for dark headers, social banners, and presentation slides.

05. Connected Nodes

API and systems friendly

Hints at software architecture, integrations, accounts, and future platform features.

06. Terminal Product Mark

developer-oriented

A more explicit software cue. Strong for technical credibility, less unique as a long-term brand.

07. Folded Seven

distinct symbol

Abstract enough to become ownable, with the seven as the main visual signature.

08. Split System Wordmark

balanced and friendly

The boxed seven becomes a reusable mark while the word stays readable at small sizes.

09. Precision Underline

most conservative

A wordmark-first route. Minimal risk, easy to apply, but less distinctive than a symbol-led mark.

10. App Tile Family

product family ready

Works if each future product gets its own icon but shares a common rm7soft parent style.
